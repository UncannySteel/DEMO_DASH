03Company — Privacy

Yours, locally.

well… mostly. here's the rest.

The plain version: what Onextap keeps on your device, the one thing that leaves it, and what only happens if you switch it on. Written down, not left to inference.

01 / On your device

Your profile lives in your browser.

Your details, your saved answers and the field mappings you have taught it are kept in the browser's own storage, chrome.storage.local, rather than on a server. No account is needed to start.

02 / What leaves it

Drafting is the one trip out.

When you ask for a written answer, the relevant text is sent to our AI providers to draft it:

  • ◆ The job posting you are applying to.
  • ◆ The parts of your profile and earlier answers the draft is built from.
  • ◆ Only the text needed to draft — nothing else leaves the device.

03 / Only if you ask

Backup is opt-in.

Premium includes encrypted cloud backup, so your profile can sync across your machines. It is off until you turn it on.

04 / Your say

Nothing goes out unread.

Every drafted answer stays editable before it is saved or submitted. Onextap fills the form; you decide what gets sent. Questions about any of this? Ask us.
